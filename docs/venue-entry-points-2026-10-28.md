# Venue entrances and walkthrough

Development only. Public domain and live database are unchanged.

## Clear entrances

- **Find venues** (`/venues`): customer discovery, shortlist, venue details and enquiries.
- **Venue owners** (`/venues/partner`): listing and workspace entrance for venue teams, directly in public navigation.
- **Venue login** (`/venue-portal`): authenticated listing, media, supplier choice, enquiries and calendar.
- **Venue preview** (`/venues/partner/preview`): interactive customer route and supplier settings demonstration. No data is saved.
- `/business/venue-routing` redirects to the venue preview for existing links. The generic business page remains a secondary directory, not the required entrance for venues.

## Customer journey

1. Search in Find venues by area, date, capacity and other preferences.
2. Open a venue to review photos, practical details, walkthrough/video (where supplied), and supplier arrangements.
3. Send a viewing or booking enquiry to the venue team, or follow its existing booking system link if available. A request does not confirm a booking.
4. Follow that venue's chosen supplier route: its own recommended network, its approved suppliers on Styled, or the wider Styled range.

## Venue team journey

1. Use Venue owners, then Start your venue listing or Venue login.
2. Save listing information and photos privately. Add an existing supported tour/video if available; photographs alone do not generate a 3D walkthrough.
3. In Suppliers & booking, choose the supplier route, list recommended businesses, set local sourcing preferences and optionally add an external booking link.
4. Submit the listing for review. Staff receive it in Venue reviews (`/admin/venues`). The venue Overview displays review state and any requested changes.
5. After approval, manage enquiries and calendar blocks in the venue workspace.

Own-network venues do not feed couples into unrelated catalogue suppliers. Recommended suppliers must separately join and be staff-linked before they can offer an approved product range through Styled. External links do not automatically synchronise availability, and distance filtering is not a sustainability certification.
